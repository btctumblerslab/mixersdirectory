## 🔍 What is Mixers Directory?

[Mixers Directory](https://mixersdirectory.com/) is an independent review site for **BTC mixers and coin tumblers**. Every service is checked by hand and scored on the things that actually matter for on-chain privacy, not on marketing claims.

Bitcoin is pseudonymous, not anonymous. Every transaction stays on a public ledger forever. A mixer breaks the visible link between incoming and outgoing coins, and choosing the right one is the hard part. That's what this directory is for.

## 🧪 How services are rated

- **Fees** — percentage range and fixed network fee
- **Logs** — stated retention policy and how long data is kept
- **Delays** — custom time delays between outputs
- **Minimum / maximum** — smallest and largest amount accepted
- **Access** — clearnet, Tor (.onion) or both
- **Letter of guarantee** — whether a signed guarantee is issued
- **Track record** — how long the service has been running

## 🛡️ Privacy tips

> [!TIP]
> - Always use the **official link** listed on the directory. Phishing clones are everywhere
> - Save the **letter of guarantee** until the mix is complete
> - Use **several output addresses** with random delays
> - Access mixers over **Tor** whenever possible
> - Don't send mixed coins straight back to a KYC exchange account
